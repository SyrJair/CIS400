Goals

  • Facilitate real-time communication between people.
  • Provide trustworthy, location-based updates about shelters, medical aid, and safety resources.
  • Reduce confusion and misinformation during disaster scenarios.

Target Users

Individuals affected by natural disasters: People who need quick access to shelters, food, medical aid, or safety updates.

Local businesses and volunteers: Those offering resources (like supplies, shelter, or transportation) who need a platform to connect safely with people in need.

Key Features

  • Interactive Map with Real-Time GPS: Shows nearby shelters, aid stations, verified help offers, and SOS alerts.
  • Live Camera Story Submissions: Real-time media capture with metadata (no gallery uploads) to ensure authenticity.
  • Account System: Profiles for individuals, businesses, and organizations to boost trust and credibility.

Heuristic Evaluation

Visibility of System Status (Medium Severity)

Users had no clear feedback on upload or approval status. We implemented a pending status update and added a confirmation after media was uploaded.

Consistency and Standards (Low Severity)

Different icons were used for similar actions. We made the icons bigger and clearly color-coded them.

User Control and Freedom (Medium Severity)

Users couldn’t cancel or retake media if the quality was poor. We added a cancel button after media was taken to offer more control.

Reflection & Conclusion

What Worked Well

  • Staying focused on user needs made a huge difference.
  • Clear, simple navigation helped users act fast during emergencies.
  • Adding real-time feedback (like confirmation messages) increased user trust.

What Could Be Improved

  • More Diverse User Testing: Future testing should include older users, rural communities, and non-English speakers.
  • Stronger Offline Support: Future versions should support offline map caching and pending help requests.

How Our Prototype Meets User Needs

  • Speed: Quick action buttons and live maps mean users find help fast.
  • Trust: Verified accounts and instant feedback give users confidence in the information they see.
  • Simplicity: Clear language and big buttons reduce confusion.

Impact of Heuristic Evaluation

  • Improved Navigation: Clear menus and “Back to Map” buttons made it easier to move around.
  • Error Prevention: Adding cancel steps before posting reduced mistakes.

Final Thoughts

Designing ResQue taught us that simple, clear, and trustworthy design is crucial when people are under stress. By applying HCI principles carefully, we created an app that could save lives by making it easier for people to connect, help, and stay safe during a disaster.
